$c[Requires Variable "UsernameWantsFriends", set it to "discord"]
$c[Supports Slash Commands! Requires Text Option "find_or_ask" when added as slash command.]

$if[$message[find_or_ask]==find]
Somebody whose username is `$getVar[UsernameWantsFriends]` Wants to be your friend!
$elseif[$message[find_or_ask]==ask]
$setVar[UsernameWantsFriends;$username]
Now anyone who says "qv!friends find" will see your username until somebody says "qv!friends ask".
$elseif[$message==find]
Somebody whose username is `$getVar[UsernameWantsFriends]` Wants to be your friend!
$elseif[$message==ask]
$setVar[UsernameWantsFriends;$username]
Now anyone who says "qv!friends find" will see your username until somebody says "qv!friends ask".
$else
Options: "find, ask"
$endif
